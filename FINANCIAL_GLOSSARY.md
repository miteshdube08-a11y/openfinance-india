
# Financial Glossary

A beginner-friendly reference for important financial and investment research terminology.

## A

### Asset
An economic resource owned or controlled by a company that may provide future economic benefits.

### Asset Turnover
A financial ratio measuring how efficiently a company uses its assets to generate revenue.

Formula:

Revenue / Average Total Assets

## B

### Balance Sheet
A financial statement showing a company's assets, liabilities, and shareholders' equity at a particular date.

### Book Value
The accounting value of a company's assets minus its liabilities.

## C

### Cash Flow
The movement of cash into and out of a business during a particular period.

### Cash Flow from Operations (CFO)
Cash generated or used by a company's core operating activities.

### Current Ratio
A liquidity ratio measuring a company's ability to meet short-term obligations.

Formula:

Current Assets / Current Liabilities

## D

### Debt-to-Equity Ratio
A financial ratio comparing a company's total debt with shareholders' equity.

Formula:

Total Debt / Shareholders' Equity

### Dividend
A distribution of company profits to shareholders, subject to the company's dividend policy and applicable rules.

## E

### Earnings Per Share (EPS)
The portion of a company's profit attributable to each outstanding ordinary share.

### EBITDA
Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortisation.

### Equity
The residual interest in a company's assets after deducting liabilities.

## F

### Free Cash Flow (FCF)
Cash available after operating cash flow and capital expenditure.

A commonly used simplified formula:

Operating Cash Flow - Capital Expenditure

## G

### Gross Profit Margin
The percentage of revenue remaining after deducting the cost of goods sold.

Formula:

(Gross Profit / Revenue) × 100

## M

### Market Capitalisation
The total market value of a company's outstanding shares.

Formula:

Share Price × Outstanding Shares

### Net Profit Margin
The percentage of revenue remaining as net profit.

Formula:

(Net Profit / Revenue) × 100

## P

### Price-to-Earnings Ratio (P/E)
A valuation ratio comparing a company's share price with its earnings per share.

Formula:

Share Price / Earnings Per Share

### Price-to-Book Ratio (P/B)
A valuation ratio comparing market value per share with book value per share.

Formula:

Market Price per Share / Book Value per Share

## R

### Return on Equity (ROE)
A profitability ratio measuring profit relative to shareholders' equity.

Formula:

Net Profit / Average Shareholders' Equity × 100

### Return on Capital Employed (ROCE)
A profitability ratio measuring operating profit relative to capital employed.

Formula:

EBIT / Capital Employed × 100

## W

### Working Capital
The difference between current assets and current liabilities.

Formula:

Current Assets - Current Liabilities

---

## Important Note

Financial ratios may vary depending on accounting definitions and the methodology used.

Always review the calculation assumptions and underlying financial statements before interpreting a ratio.
